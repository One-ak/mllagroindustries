Privacy Notice

This notice explains how contact and inquiry information submitted through this website is handled.

Last updated: 16 July 2026

Information we receive

The inquiry form collects the name, phone number, inquiry category and message that you choose to provide. Technical security logs may record limited request information such as time and network address to protect the service from misuse.

How the information is used

Submitted information is used to route and respond to product, distributorship, vendor, export, career and customer-support inquiries. It may also be used to maintain an operational record of the communication and to protect the website from spam or abuse.

Who may receive it

Information may be shared internally with the relevant Vansh Group company or team handling the inquiry. It may also be processed by service providers that support website hosting or business communication, subject to appropriate confidentiality and security expectations. Inquiry information is not sold.

Retention and protection

Inquiry records are retained only for as long as reasonably needed for communication, operational follow-up, dispute handling and applicable record-keeping needs. Access should be limited to authorized personnel, and reasonable technical safeguards are used to protect stored information.

Your choices

You may ask about your submitted information, request a correction, or ask that it be deleted when continued retention is not required. Do not submit confidential business formulas, financial credentials, identity documents or other sensitive information through the general inquiry form.

Contact

For a privacy-related request, email vanshgroupofficial@gmail.com with enough detail to identify the relevant inquiry. You may also call +91 9670252525.
